# Pentatonica

A 2048-style puzzle where each tile represents a musical note. Slide the tiles across the 4×4 board
to merge two equal notes into the next note in the pentatonic scale, moving up from C1 to C4.

- Every time you merge two notes, they play their respective notes, so each game turns into a
  melody.
- Seven musical cultures, each with their own scale and instrument: Celtic harp, Chinese guzheng,
  Japanese koto, Indian sitar, Middle Eastern oud, Indonesian gamelan, and American guitar.
- Combos: merging on consecutive swipes raises the score multiplier up to x3.
- A shared leaderboard for all time and for the last 30 days.
- The game is available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Ukrainian.

## Play

- **Browser:** [pentatonica.pp.ua](https://pentatonica.pp.ua)
- **Windows:** download `Pentatonica-PC.zip` from the
  [latest release](https://github.com/Mardivil/Pentatonica/releases/latest), unpack it anywhere
  and run `Pentatonica.exe`. Nothing to install, Java is included.
- **Android 13 or later:** download `Pentatonica.apk` from the
  [latest release](https://github.com/Mardivil/Pentatonica/releases/latest) on your phone and open
  it. Allow your browser to install apps when asked.

Swipe on a touch screen, or use the arrow keys or WASD.

Privacy policy: [pentatonica.pp.ua/privacy.html](https://pentatonica.pp.ua/privacy.html)
