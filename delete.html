Pentatonica leaderboard: delete your entry

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Pentatonica by Mardivil keeps a leaderboard entry for each player who chose a name: the name, the best score of all time and the best score of each of the last 30 days. Deleting it removes all of that from the server at once. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Delete in the game

Open Settings and, under Player code, choose Delete me from the leaderboard.

Delete on this website

Enter your player code. The game shows it in Settings, under Player code. The code is the only proof that the entry is yours, so an entry cannot be deleted without it.
